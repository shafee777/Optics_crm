2 Google Drive Cloud Sync Setup (Recommended & Easiest)

By linking Google Drive on your computer, your backups are automatically uploaded to the cloud the moment you click Save in Optics CRM.

Step A: One-Time Google Drive Setup
  • Install Google Drive for Desktop from google.com/drive/download.
  • Log in with your Gmail / Google account.
  • Windows creates a Drive folder (e.g., G:\My Drive or Google Drive).
  • Inside it, create a new folder: Optics CRM Backups.
Step B: Saving to Google Drive
  • Open Optics CRM and log in as Owner.
  • Go to Settings → Desktop backups.
  • Click the green "Save backup" button.
  • Select your Optics CRM Backups folder inside Google Drive.
  • Click Save. Google Drive syncs it to cloud immediately!
Double Protection: Optics CRM also automatically maintains a local backup on your computer (inside %APPDATA%\Optics CRM\backups). This means you always have both a local copy and a cloud copy!

3 Reinstallation & How to Restore Data
Scenario A: Reinstalling on the SAME Computer

When you uninstall and reinstall on the same PC using the installer from desktop/release:

  • The installer preserves your local data folder automatically.
  • Open the newly installed app — it will open directly to your regular Login Screen.
  • Enter your existing Owner email & password. All 300+ products and 1,000+ customers are immediately there!
Scenario B: Restoring on a NEW Computer (or after formatting Windows)

When moving to a fresh computer or completely wiped system:

  • Install Optics CRM on the new computer and launch it.
  • Because it is brand new, you will see the "Welcome to Optics CRM: Set up your shop" screen.
  • Enter any quick temporary shop name, email, and password to enter the app.
  • Go to Settings → Desktop backups.
  • Click "Restore backup" and select your .dump file (from your USB drive or Google Drive).
  • Click "Restore and restart" in the confirmation box.
  • The app automatically restores all records and restarts.
  • On the login screen, enter your Original Owner Email and Password.
  • Done! Everything is restored in full.
4 Important Rules & Mistakes to Avoid
Common Mistake What to Do Instead
Unplugging USB drive too early Always wait until you see the confirmation message: "Backup saved. Keep a copy on a separate drive." before removing your flash drive.
Manually copying AppData folder Do not copy system folders manually. Credentials are encrypted per Windows profile. Always use the built-in "Save backup" button.
Confusing CSV Export with Backup CSV Export is for viewing data in Excel / filing taxes. To restore software data, always use the .dump file from Desktop backups.
Forgetting the Owner Password After restoring, you must log in with your original owner password. Keep your password noted in a secure notebook.
